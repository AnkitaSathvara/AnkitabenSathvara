# Scenario 1: Computer Not Turning On

## Problem
User reports that the computer does not turn on when pressing the power button.

## Possible Causes
- Power cable not connected
- Faulty power supply
- Battery issue (for laptops)
- Hardware failure

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check power cable and connections
2. Try a different power outlet
3. Check if power supply is receiving power
4. For laptops, remove battery and try direct power
5. Test with another charger or cable

## Outcome
The system powered on after reconnecting the power cable properly.
